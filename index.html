Data validation notice: The 20 EV/ICE vehicle ratings in this build are working estimates carried forward from the initial comparison, not configuration-verified EPA records. Each information icon links to the official EPA vehicle finder, not an individual confirmed record. No live gasoline-price service is connected. Replace these values and links with validated 2024–2026 records before treating the results as publication-grade.
Method: EV cost per mile = effective charger price ÷ EV mi/kWh. Equivalent gasoline price = EV cost per mile × ICE MPG. Under proportional adjustment, ICE MPG = reference ICE MPG × (observed EV mi/kWh ÷ reference EV mi/kWh). Effective charger rate = [(billed kWh × rate + session fees) × (1 + applicable tax)] ÷ billed kWh. Electricity and gasoline energy costs only; excludes ownership and maintenance costs.
Official reference: EPA/DOE Find a Car · EPA data service · AAA gas averages.
